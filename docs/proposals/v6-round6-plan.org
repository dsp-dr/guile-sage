* Boundary-probe checklist (RFC-004 §17 content = the coverage matrix)
Write-path *fail-open hazards* are the crown jewels — *A2, B3, C1* — where the
typed-vs-untyped control earns its keep.

*A. Canonical action:* A1 determinism (pure/total, equal⇒byte-identical) · A2
no-alias *(hazard: collision ⇒ policy-equiv)* · A3 idempotence · A4 coverage (every
mutating tool; safe = distinct read-class) · A5 reuse-don't-relax (§3 pure guard +
coercion).
*B. Verify vs policy:* B1 partition/totality {allow,confirm,deny} · B2 monotonicity ·
B3 value-gated *(hazard, §15.3)* · B4 default-deny for mutation · B5 write-path
no-oracle (deny doesn't distinguish policy-deny from unknown).
*C. Attestation:* C1 no-unattested-mutation *(hazard; fail-closed write)* · C2
append-only + total order (Pony) · C3 tamper-evident chain (§7 sha) · C4 log
round-trip (§15.6/§15.7) · C5 taint interplay (store digest, not raw bytes) · C6
own-LLM-path only (§4 served-plain extends to the log) · C7 query is read-only +
unforgeable.
*D. v6 clarity carry-items* (see [[file:v6-clarity-worklist.org][worklist]]): D1 §16.1/2 strings hold under the
canonical layer · D2 canonicalization uses the *pure* safe-path verdict (§16.4) ·
D3 deterministic encoding, sentinel {}≠[]≠null≠absent (§16.3).
